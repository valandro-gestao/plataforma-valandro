Primary call-to-action button, used for the single most important action on a screen.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="md" onClick={handleSubmit}>Falar com um consultor</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (blue, main CTA), `secondary` (navy, secondary emphasis), `outline` (bordered, low emphasis), `ghost` (text-only, tertiary). Sizes: `sm`, `md`, `lg`. Use at most one `primary` button per view.
